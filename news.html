  • March 2026
    Funding

    Dean's Applied AI Faculty Scholarly Fellowship awarded to the lab.

    Marie received one of the College of Engineering's inaugural Dean's Applied AI Faculty Scholarly Fellowships, supporting work titled Building AI Expertise to Transform Quantitative Ultrasound and Reveal Hidden Tissue Information. The project integrates AI-based signal and image analysis with the lab's existing physics-based scattering models — learning directly from raw ultrasound waves to surface tissue features that conventional approaches miss.
